Learning a language is not just remembering translations. It is recognizing a character in a sentence, hearing a familiar sound, and knowing when to use a phrase.

Chino 100 brings those moments together for Spanish-speaking Mandarin learners.

## Start with something you can use

The curriculum focuses on HSK 1–2 vocabulary and everyday structures. Character practice, listening, visual questions, and short readings connect different ways of understanding the same material.

Pinyin stays hidden until you ask for it. That small choice encourages you to look at the character first, while keeping help within reach.

## Practice without handing over your progress

Progress is saved on your device. Practice and grading follow defined rules, rather than asking a language model to decide whether an answer is correct.

Optional local AI can help vary wording. It is not the source of the curriculum or the authority on your answers, and compatible hardware and a model download are needed to use it.

> The goal is not to make every exercise different. It is to make each attempt a little more meaningful.

Explore the project, try a lesson, and build understanding one small step at a time.
