Recognizing a solution is easier than producing one. A tutorial can feel clear while you read it, then disappear from memory when the editor is empty.

DSA Mastery starts with that gap.

## Recall before you run

Active recall asks you to retrieve an idea before seeing it again. Explain the pattern, write a small Python attempt, and use the result to decide what to revisit.

The browser runs Python in a separate worker so the learning interface does not have to share its execution loop. Progress stays on your device.

## Hints should leave room to think

Deterministic feedback is the foundation. Optional browser AI can offer another wording of a hint, but it should not replace the evaluator or reveal a full solution.

This is deliberately different from asking an assistant to finish the task for you.

## Look for the reusable idea

A sliding window, a traversal, or a pair of pointers is more than a memorized snippet. The important question is when the pattern applies and why it works.

One-DSA provides a wider library and roadmap. DSA Mastery gives you a focused place to turn those ideas into practice.
