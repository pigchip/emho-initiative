Useful knowledge becomes more valuable when more people can reach it. That is the starting point of EMHO Initiative: small, practical learning tools with clear limits and room for collaboration.

## Free access is a beginning

Removing a price or an account requirement makes a tool easier to try. Accessible design, transparent behavior, and understandable content matter just as much.

We aim to keep core learning independent of cloud AI subscriptions. Optional on-device models are an enhancement, not a condition for learning.

## Open code needs clear rights

Source code being visible does not automatically make it open source. A project's license determines whether and how its code can be reused. Content, fonts, recordings, and model weights can have separate terms.

Check those terms before building on a project.

## Public-good ambition, honest claims

EMHO is an initiative inspired by sharing knowledge as a public resource. The projects featured here are not presented as certified entries in the Digital Public Goods Alliance registry.

Our direction is evidence-informed learning, multiple perspectives, human judgment, and open collaboration. Those are commitments to work toward, not a substitute for evidence of impact.
